## Role in the neck (user photo 2026-11-04 + inference — confirm before building the URDF)

- This part is the **new link between J3 and J4**, replacing the SO101 `Lower_Arm` (J3→J4 = 134.9 mm in upstream URDF).
  Fork = on the J3 horn (J3 axis ∥ part Y). Socket = holds the J4 motor body.
- STS3215 in the upstream meshes: 45.4 × 24.8 × 39.6 (shaft ∥ the 39.6 side, shaft 10.2 mm from one end). The pocket
  takes the 24.8 side along Y and the 35 side along Z, so **the J4 shaft is ∥ Z, i.e. perpendicular to J3**
  (in SO101 J3 ∥ J4). **Confirmed by user 2026-11-04: J4 swings the camera sideways.** The downstream SO101 wrist
  (J4→J6) is therefore rotated 90° about the link axis relative to stock SO101.
  If the non-shaft end sits on the pocket floor, the J4 axis passes through (X ≈ −20.2, Y 23.9),
  ≈ 61 mm from the J3 axis along X (position not yet measured on the real part).
- Camera end: D435i holder from upstream XLeRobot on the J6 horn. CAD available in
  `third_party/XLeRobot/hardware/step/RGBD_Gimbal/Gimbal_Pitch_Holder_d435_d415.step` and
  `third_party/XLeRobot/hardware/camera_connector/Gimbal_mesh_all_d435.stl`; upstream URDF puts `head_camera_link`
  at (0.025, 0, 0.03) m from the head tilt axis (rounded values).
- Rest of the chain (J1/J2 base + shoulder, upper arm, wrist, J6 housing) looks like stock SO101 in the photo.

## Calibration — `tools/calib_neck.py`

Hand-guided, torque never enabled. `guide` renders `calib_guide.png` (zero pose + the `+30°` direction of every joint),
`calibrate` records per joint: zero (homing so it reads 2047), sign (you turn it the way the guide shows), range
(unwrapped, so J5 may cross the encoder wrap; > 1 rev is clipped symmetrically) → EEPROM offset re-centred on the range
midpoint + limits minus a 40-tick margin, and `neck_calibration.json` (`q = sign·(Present_Position − zero_tick)·2π/4096`).
Old EEPROM values are backed up to `calib_backups/` and restored on any failure or Ctrl+C. `build_urdf.py` takes the
joint limits from `neck_calibration.json` when it exists. `show` reads the encoders and renders `live_pose.png`.
Tested offline against a simulated bus (wrap, reversed signs, J5 > 1 rev, bad reads).
The input-voltage status error on IDs 9/10/11 that blocked LeRobot was cleared 2026-11-04 (Max_Voltage_Limit 12.0 → 14.0 V,
see `hardware.yaml` → `eeprom_20261104_evening.fix_20261104`).
